Walkthrough

How to replace text in a PDF

The fastest way to swap out a line of text: paint over it, then type the replacement. Here's the whole flow.

Step 1

Open your PDF

Drag a file onto the drop zone, or click it to choose one from your computer. It opens instantly — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Step 2

Draw tool → White → paint over the text

Switch to the Draw tool in the left rail, pick White from the Ink swatches, turn the marker size up, and paint a stroke over the words you want gone.

Step 3

Switch to the Text tool

Click Text in the rail, then click the blank white patch you just painted.

Step 4

Type your new text

A text box drops in right there — start typing immediately. It behaves like a normal text field.

Step 5

Style it — font, size, color

With the text box selected, the Text style panel appears on the right: pick a font, set the exact size, or open the color wheel for any color you like.

Step 6

Drag it into place

Hover the text box and grab the small round handle at its corner — drag it anywhere on the page. Works no matter which tool is active.

Step 7

Download your PDF

Click Download for a free copy with a small watermark, or remove the watermark by watching a short ad — no sign-up, no payment.
